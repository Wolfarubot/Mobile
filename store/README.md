# Store listing art

Graphics for the Google Play (and later App Store) listing. They aren't part of the game build.

| Folder | What | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `icon/` | App icon (the pixel sword) | 512×512 PNG |
| `feature-graphics/` | Three feature graphic options: the party with the Demon Lord behind, a graveyard battlefield, and a Hunters-vs-Guardian showdown | 1024×500 PNG |
| `screenshots-stylized/` | Eight marketing screenshots: a captioned, tilted phone with a ribbon across it and the game's sprites around it | 1080×1920 PNG |
| `screenshots-plain/` | Seventeen plain screenshots straight from the game: battlefields, Guardian fights, a swarm, gear, skills, events, the world map and six minigames | 1080×1920 PNG |

Google Play takes up to 8 phone screenshots (at least 2), each under 8 MB with a long side of at most twice the short
side. All of these fit. Captions use the game's title font (Pixelify Sans, bold) with a heavy black outline, as on the
loading screen and in the game's text.
