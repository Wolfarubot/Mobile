Minigame art, all from CraftPix.net (license: https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/):
- grave-patch.png: the round patch of cobblestones from "Free Undead Tileset Top Down Pixel Art" (Ground_rocks.png,
  at 387,177), the ground each spot in Alias's Grave Robbing stands on.
- stone-btn.png, stone-btn-down.png (buttons 7 and 10) and arrow-up/down/left/right.png (icons 26, 27, 25, 24)
  from the "Free Pixel Art Platformer UI" pack: Grave Robbing's D-pad.
- path-lane.png, path-lane-ok.png: the vertical path from CraftPix's "Free Path and Road Top Down Pixel Tileset"
  (Road5_grass.png and Road1_grass.png, at 184,208), trimmed and widened (12 columns from inside the road repeated,
  so the road is 30px): Pixie Serenade's paths (pale cobbles, and blue cobbles over the okay range), tinted per path
  in the game. Its perfect-spot bars are the event timers' framed bar (public/ui/timer-bar.png).
- note.png: icon 50 (a music note) from the "Free Pixel Art Platformer UI" pack, tinted per path in Pixie Serenade.
- eye.png: Eyes_shadow2_2 from CraftPix's "Free Cursed Land Top Down Pixel Art" objects: the eyes in Wilhelm's
  Shooting Gallery (edges faded in the game, and recoloured red for the red eyes).
- lantern-1..6.png: torches_1, 6, 11, 16, 21 and 26 from the "Top Down Dungeon Pixel Tileset" (a ghost-flame brazier,
  its six frames): the Shooting Gallery's lanterns.
- crosshair.png: icon 9 (a ring) from the "Free Pixel Art Platformer UI" pack with four ticks and a centre dot added:
  the Shooting Gallery's crosshair.
- ledge.png: a chunk of raised earth (flat top, roots hanging below) from CraftPix's "Free Cave Tileset" ground
  tiles (Ground.png, at 612,20): the ledge Wilhelm stands on in the Shooting Gallery.
- forge-vent.png: Volcano1 from CraftPix's "Cave Tileset Top Down" objects (as in public/backgrounds/caves): the fire
  vent beside Kargesh's anvil in Forge Strike. The anvil, ingot, hammer and finished weapons are icons from the icon
  packs, and the strike bar is the event timers' framed bar with the platformer UI's down arrow as its marker.
- crack1..3.png: three of the ice cracks from CraftPix's "Winter Top Down Pixel Art Tileset" (Tiled_files/cracks.png, at
  272,16; 272,112 and 16,112), the cracks alone (the ice cut away) recoloured as dark earth: the ground splitting open
  in Grave Robbing where a skeleton is about to burst out, small to gaping as it nears.
- arrow.png: one arrow (7 x 21, pointing down) cut from the arrow volley effect (public/effects/arrows.png, frame 3 at
  181,23; "Magic Spells Pixel Art Sprite Pack", Magic/3): Galladair's arrows in Hunting Practice, turned to fly.
- icicle.png: the falling ice shard (15 x 31) from the ice effect's first frame (public/effects/ice.png at 29,1;
  "Magic Spells Pixel Art Sprite Pack", Magic/5): Icebreaker's icicles (the effect's last frames are their shatter).
- cards.png: a playing card sheet provided with the game's assets: 56 x 80 cards, 15 to a row (jokers, backs, then
  Ace to King), rows of hearts, spades, diamonds and clubs (and two recoloured suits): Ted's High or Low.
Everything else the minigames draw comes from the packs already in the game: icons (public/icons), monster and Hunter
sheets, magic effects (public/effects), the RPG UI's panel, button and framed bar (public/ui), and the areas' scenery
pieces (public/backgrounds: the Meteor Field's rocks and shooting stars, the Steps' floating island, the Frost Peaks'
rocks). Only lighting is drawn in code: shadows, glows, fades, shot tracers and the odd sparkle.
