Pocket Hunter Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Pocket Hunter game (the "app", for Android, iOS and the web) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Pocket Hunter is made by Variette Studios ("we", "us").

In short: you can play without an account, and then your game is saved only on your device. If you sign in, we store your email address and your save in the cloud so it follows you between devices. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. We don't sell your information.

Information we collect

Without an account

Your game progress is saved on your device only. We don't receive it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Google, Apple or an email address and password. We then collect:

  • Account details: your email address, an account ID, and which sign-in method you used. With Google or Apple sign-in, your name may also be shared with us by that provider. With Apple, you can choose to hide your email address, and Apple then gives us a private relay address instead. We never see your password: email sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your cloud save: your game progress (levels, items, areas and similar game data) and when it was last saved, so you can continue on another device.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Submit Feedback, we receive your message along with technical details to help us fix problems: the app version, your platform, your screen size, your browser or device's user-agent string, and a summary of your game progress (such as your current area and level). Feedback is not linked to your account. Please don't include personal information in your message.

Purchases

If you buy something in the app, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We don't receive your card or payment details; we only learn that the purchase went through so we can deliver it.

Technical data

Like any internet service, the services we use (listed below) receive your IP address and basic device information when the app connects to them. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your game saved across devices.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free.
  • To read and answer feedback, and to fix bugs.
  • To keep the game secure and prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the services that run parts of the game:

All information is sent over encrypted connections, and only your own account can read or change your cloud save.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the Menu, choose Account, then Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and your cloud save. A copy of your game stays on your device until you uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can ask us to delete your account by contacting us (below) from the email address you signed in with. We will delete it within 30 days.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and cloud save: until you delete your account.
  • Feedback messages: as long as they're useful for improving the game, and no longer than two years.
  • Ad data: as set out in Google's policies.

Your rights and choices

  • Play without signing in, so nothing about your game leaves your device.
  • Reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, and to object to how it's used. Contact us to make a request.

Children

Pocket Hunter is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: contact Variette Studios at the developer email address shown on Pocket Hunter's Google Play or App Store page, or use Settings → Submit Feedback in the app.
